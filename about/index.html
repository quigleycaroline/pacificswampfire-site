About

Pacific swampfire (Salicornia pacifica, previously classified as Sarcocornia pacifica) is a low-growing, succulent perennial plant native to coastal salt marshes, tidal flats, and alkaline soils along the Pacific coast of North America.

It is also the photography project of Caroline Quigley, an artist and writer based in California. Her photographs capture both sweeping landscapes and the life they sustain, shot on film across the American West.
